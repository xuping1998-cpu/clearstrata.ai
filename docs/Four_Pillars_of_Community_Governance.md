## Purpose

Define the **Four Pillars** that constitute the constitutional experience of ClearStrata — the framework through which every governance feature is evaluated.

Communities do not become stronger simply by making decisions. They become stronger by **remembering how** those decisions were made, **why** they were made, and **what they learned**.

## Constitutional Meaning

Communities **do not** become stronger simply by **making decisions**.

Communities become stronger by **remembering**:

- **How** those decisions were made
- **Why** they were made
- **What** they learned

The Four Pillars encode this journey: **listen → think → decide → learn**.

---

## Repository Principle

These **Four Pillars** constitute the **constitutional experience** of ClearStrata.

| Rule | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **Strengthen** | Every future feature should strengthen **at least one** pillar |
| **Do not weaken** | No feature should weaken **any** pillar |

Constitution Review shall map each Feature Proposal to the pillar(s) it serves.

---

### 社区治理四大支柱

共同倾听。共同思考。共同决定。共同成长。

未来所有功能，都应强化其中 **至少一项**。  
任何功能，都 **不得削弱** 任何一项。
