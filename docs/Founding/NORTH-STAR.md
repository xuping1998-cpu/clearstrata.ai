## North Star Definition

The North Star is the **permanent purpose** of the ClearStrata System.

It exists **above** all governance policies, architectural decisions, requirements, milestones, and implementations.

Every decision made within the ClearStrata System **shall move the platform closer to its North Star**.

The North Star guides:

- Governance
- Architecture
- Engineering
- Artificial Intelligence
- Product Evolution
- Community Development

Technology will continue to evolve.  
Artificial Intelligence will continue to evolve.  
Robotics will continue to evolve.

The methods may change.  
The implementation may change.  
The technologies may change.

**The North Star shall remain unchanged.**

---

## 北極星定義

北極星是 ClearStrata 系統 **永久不變的存在目的**。

它 **高於** 所有治理政策、架構決策、需求變更、里程碑、以及所有技術實現。

ClearStrata 系統中的 **每一項決策**，都應使平台 **更接近北極星**。

北極星指引：

- 社區治理
- 系統架構
- 工程方法
- 人工智慧
- 產品演進
- 社區發展

科技會持續演進。  
人工智慧會持續演進。  
機器人會持續演進。

實現方式可以改變。  
技術可以改變。  
產品可以演進。

**唯有北極星永不改變。**

## Constitutional Principle

The North Star is **not a slogan**.

It is the **highest guiding principle** of the ClearStrata System.

Every constitutional document, every governance decision, every architectural decision, every requirement, every milestone, and every implementation **shall remain aligned** with the North Star.

---

## 憲法原則

北極星 **不是一句口號**。

它是 ClearStrata 系統 **最高的指導原則**。

所有憲章、治理決策、架構決策、需求變更、里程碑、以及所有技術實現，都應 **始終與北極星保持一致**。
