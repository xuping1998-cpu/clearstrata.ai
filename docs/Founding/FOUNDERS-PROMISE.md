## Founder's Promise

Technology is never the purpose.  
**Communities are.**

Artificial Intelligence is never the governor.  
**People are.**

Automation is never the objective.  
**Better communities are.**

We will never adopt technology simply because it is newer.

We will adopt technology **only when** it helps communities become:

- More transparent.
- More trustworthy.
- More democratic.
- More efficient.
- More sustainable.

Every technological advancement must ultimately improve the lives of the people it serves.

---

## 創始承諾

科技從來不是目的。**社區才是。**

人工智慧從來不是治理者。**人民才是。**

自動化從來不是目標。**更美好的社區才是。**

我們不會因為技術更新、更熱門、更先進，就採用它。

我們只會在技術能夠真正幫助社區變得：

- 更加公開透明
- 更加值得信任
- 更加民主自治
- 更加高效協同
- 更加永續發展

時，才採用它。

每一次技術進步，最終都應改善它所服務的人們、社區，以及未來世代的生活品質。

---

## Constitutional Commitment

ClearStrata shall always place:

- **Communities** before technology.
- **People** before automation.
- **Trust** before efficiency.
- **Transparency** before convenience.
- **Long-term sustainability** before short-term gain.

Artificial Intelligence shall **enhance** human judgment, **never replace** community self-governance.

---

## 憲章承諾

ClearStrata 永遠堅持：

- **社區優先於技術。**
- **人民優先於自動化。**
- **信任優先於效率。**
- **透明優先於便利。**
- **長遠永續優先於短期利益。**

人工智慧應 **增強** 人類的判斷能力，**而不是取代** 社區的自治能力。
