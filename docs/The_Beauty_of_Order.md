## Purpose

This principle explains **why** every permanent document must preserve explicit constitutional relationships through **Related Documents**.

It exists to guard **institutional memory**, **traceability**, and the **ordered beauty** of the ClearStrata repository — not merely to improve navigation.

---

## Scope

**In scope:**

- The constitutional rationale for mandatory **Related Documents**
- The repository design obligation that no official document exist in isolation
- All permanent official documents (FD, CA, CS, GP, GC, CR, AI, AR, PR, RM, TM per [CS-001](Repository_Document_Numbering_Standard.md))

**Out of scope:**

- The technical format of the Identity Block (see [CS-002](Document_Identity_Block_Standard.md))
- Section ordering and layout rules (see [CS-003](Repository_Document_Layout_Standard.md))
- Individual document content and governance substance

## The Beauty of Order

Every official document belongs to a **larger constitutional ecosystem**.

No constitutional document shall exist **in isolation**.

Every official document shall explicitly acknowledge:

- The documents **upon which it depends**
- The documents **it supports**

For this reason, **Related Documents** is a **mandatory** part of every permanent document.

This requirement exists **not merely for navigation**, but to preserve:

- **Constitutional relationships**
- **Institutional memory**
- **The beauty of an ordered repository**

---

### 秩序之美

每一份正式文档，都属于整个《平台宪章》治理体系的一部分。

没有任何一份正式文档，是孤立存在的。

每一份正式文档，都应明确说明：

- 它建立在哪些文献之上
- 又将支撑哪些文献的发展

因此，**Related Documents** 不是为了方便跳转，而是正式文档的 **强制组成部分**。

它的存在，是为了维护：

- 宪章之间的关联
- 制度之间的连续
- 知识之间的传承
- 以及整个 Repository 的 **秩序之美**

---

## Mandatory Rule

Every permanent document **shall contain** a **Related Documents** section.

**No exception.**

Related Documents appears **inside the Document Identity Block** per [CS-002](Document_Identity_Block_Standard.md) and [CS-003](Repository_Document_Layout_Standard.md).

---

### 强制规则

所有永久正式文档，**必须包含 Related Documents**。

**没有例外。**
