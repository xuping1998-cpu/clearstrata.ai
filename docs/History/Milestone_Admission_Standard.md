## Purpose

This standard defines which achievements may be admitted into [The Repository Hall of Milestones](Milestones.md) (RM-006).

The Hall of Milestones is reserved for events that permanently influence the evolution of ClearStrata.

---

## Fundamental Principle

Not every project is a milestone.  
Not every release is a milestone.  
Not every feature is a milestone.

A **Milestone** represents a permanent advancement in the constitutional, governance, or historical evolution of ClearStrata.

---

## Admission Criteria

A milestone shall satisfy **at least one** of the following criteria.

### ① Constitutional Significance

Introduces a new constitutional principle, article, governance doctrine, or institutional rule.

### ② Governance Advancement

Changes how communities govern together. Introduces a new governance capability or governance workflow.

### ③ Platform Capability

Introduces a platform capability that fundamentally expands what ClearStrata can accomplish.

### ④ Community Impact

Improves transparency, participation, accountability, institutional memory, or long-term community trust.

### ⑤ Historical Significance

Represents a defining moment in the history of the platform. Future generations should understand why it happened.

---

## Exclusion Rules

The following shall **NOT** be admitted as Milestones:

- Routine bug fixes
- Routine UI improvements
- Performance optimizations
- Dependency upgrades
- Code refactoring
- Minor feature additions
- Documentation corrections

---

## Admission Process

```
Step 1 — Complete the project
    ↓
Step 2 — Evaluate against RM-007 (this standard)
    ↓
Step 3 — If approved, assign Milestone Number (Document Registry)
    ↓
Step 4 — Register inside Repository Hall of Milestones (RM-006)
    ↓
Step 5 — Reference the related Project Record, Registry, and Chronicle
```

## The Three Golden Rules of Milestones

### Golden Rule I

A Milestone is not measured by the amount of work completed.  
It is measured by the amount of **future** it creates.

**Milestone 的价值，不取决于完成了多少工作。**  
**而取决于它为未来创造了多少可能。**

---

### Golden Rule II

A Milestone does not celebrate what was built.  
It records **what changed forever**.

**Milestone 不是为了庆祝完成了什么。**  
**而是为了记录哪些事情从此改变了历史。**

---

### Golden Rule III

Every Milestone shall leave the next generation with greater clarity than it inherited.

If the future better understands why we made today's decisions, the Milestone has fulfilled its purpose.

**每一个 Milestone，都应当让下一代建设者比我们今天更加清楚：为什么当初做出了这些决定。**

**如果未来的人能够理解今天，这个 Milestone 就完成了自己的使命。**

---

## Repository Closing Principle

**History does not remember every step.**  
**History remembers the steps that changed the direction of the journey.**

**历史不会记住每一步。**  
**历史只会记住那些改变方向的脚步。**

---

## Repository Principle

Milestones record **direction**, not activity.

History should remember **turning points**, not every step.

---

Milestone 记录的是平台发展的方向，而不是开发过程中的每一步。

**Many things improve a platform.**  
**Only a few change its history.**

---

**很多事情能够改善平台。**  
**只有少数事情能够改变历史。**
