Today marks the formal launch of **Project Zero**.

Communities are not defined by buildings.

It should help communities govern themselves better.

## Article I — Transparent Governance

Every important community decision shall be **transparent**, **traceable**, **discussable**, and **auditable**.

每一项影响社区的重要决策，都必须 **公开透明**、**全程可追溯**、**充分讨论**，并接受 **长期审计**。

**This is the platform's highest principle.**

---

## Article II — Discussion Before Decision

Every important decision should begin with **open discussion** before entering a formal resolution or vote.

每一项重要决策，都应 **先公开讨论**，再形成 **Resolution**，最后进入正式会议与投票。

---

## Article III — AI Assists, People Decide

**AI assists every decision.**  
**AI never replaces lawful decision makers.**

AI 应协助每一个决策。  
但 **绝不取代** 依法拥有决策权的人。

---

## Article IV — Evidence Before Opinion

```
Evidence
    ↓
Discussion
    ↓
Opinion
```

所有重大事项：**证据优先，意见其次。**

---

## Article V — Public by Default

Except where law requires confidentiality, governance shall be **public by default** — **supervisable**, **queryable**, and **traceable**.

除法律要求保密外，治理应 **默认公开**、可监督、可查询、可追溯。

---

## Article X — Legitimacy Before Intelligence

### The Principle of Constitutional Authority

Intelligence alone does not grant authority.  
Capability alone does not grant legitimacy.

The ability to analyze, predict, recommend, or automate decisions  
does **not** confer the right to make those decisions.

Legitimacy arises from **law**,  
the **Constitution**,  
the **shared principles of the community**,  
and the **collective decisions** made through legitimate governance.

Artificial Intelligence may assist every stage of governance.  
It shall **never replace** the constitutional authority of the community.

No matter how intelligent future systems become,  
they remain **servants of the Constitution**, not its authors.

Technology may strengthen governance.  
It shall **never become the source of governance**.

---

### 第十条 — 合法性先于智能

#### 宪章权威原则

智慧本身，并不赋予权力。  
能力本身，并不赋予合法性。

能够分析、预测、建议、甚至自动执行决策，  
并不意味着拥有作出这些决策的权力。

真正的合法性，来自 **法律**、  
来自 **《平台宪章》**、  
来自 **社区共同遵循的原则**、  
以及 **依法形成的共同决议**。

人工智能可以参与治理的每一个环节，  
但不能取代社区依法拥有的治理权。

无论未来的人工智能多么先进，  
它始终是 **《平台宪章》的执行者**，而不是 **《平台宪章》的制定者**。

技术可以增强治理。  
但不能成为 **治理合法性的来源**。

## Project Zero Rules (Repository Governance)

### Rule 0 — Build Principles Before Features

**先建立原则，再开发功能。**  
Any feature must conform to this Constitution before implementation.

### Rule 1 — Constitutional Supremacy

**The ClearStrata Constitution is the highest governing document of the platform.**

When any feature, workflow, database schema, API, AI behavior, or product decision conflicts with the Constitution, **the Constitution prevails**.

《ClearStrata 平台宪章》是平台最高治理规范。任何设计、数据库、代码、AI、功能、产品需求，若与平台宪章冲突，**一律以平台宪章为准**。

### Rule 2 — Constitution Review First

No development until **Constitution Review** is complete. See template above and [`02_Governance_Model.md`](02_Governance_Model.md).

### Rule 3 — Constitution Citation

All Feature Proposals, Architecture Designs, ADRs, PRs, and Technical Proposals must open with:

**Constitution Reference:** e.g. Article I, Article II, Article VI.

Design discussions cite articles — not merely opinion.

### Rule 4 — Constitution Before Code (AI Assistants)

All AI assistants (Cursor, Claude, GPT, Gemini) must verify constitutional compliance **before** proposing architecture, database, API, workflow, AI, or UI — and recommend design revision instead of code when non-compliant.

See: [`.cursor/rules/clearstrata-constitution.mdc`](../.cursor/rules/clearstrata-constitution.mdc)

### Rule 5 — No Constitutional Regression

No release may sacrifice **Transparency**, **Participation**, **Accountability**, **Community Memory**, or **Community Civilization** for efficiency, cost, time, or commercial pressure. **Revise the design — not the Constitution.**

### Rule 6 — Constitution Amendment

The Constitution may be amended only through the governance lifecycle it defines:

```
Proposal → Discussion → AI Summary → Constitution Amendment → Approval → Archive
```

Amending the Constitution must follow the Constitution.

### Rule 7 — Repository Order

```
README
    ↓
The Constitution
    ↓
Architecture & Governance docs
    ↓
Database / Backend / Frontend / API / Deployment
```

The Constitution always sits at the highest layer.

### Rule 8 — Project Zero Never Ends

Project Zero is not a one-time task — it is the lifecycle of this repository.

Every release note should lead with **Constitution Compliance**, e.g.:

```
Version 3.2 — Constitution Compliance
✓ Article I — Improved governance transparency
✓ Article VI — Major matters lifecycle
✓ Article VII — Community memory preservation
```

## Meta Rules

Meta Rules sit above individual features and implementations. They govern **how** the platform evolves while the Constitution governs **what** must never change.

### Meta Rule II — Never Lose the Why

#### The Principle of Purpose

Every generation of contributors will improve the platform.  
Every generation will introduce new technologies.  
Every generation will solve new problems.

Yet before changing any **architecture**, **workflow**, **feature**, or **artificial intelligence**, one question must always be asked:

> **"Does this bring ClearStrata closer to its Mission?"**

If the answer is **no**, the proposal should be **reconsidered**.

Because progress is not measured by the number of features,  
the complexity of technology,  
or the intelligence of machines.

Progress is measured by how faithfully the platform fulfills the **purpose for which it was created**.

**Technology serves the Mission.**  
**The Mission serves Communities.**  
**Communities shape Civilization.**

---

#### 元规则 II — 永远不要忘记为什么开始

##### 初心原则

未来，每一代贡献者，都会持续完善平台。  
每一代，都会引入新的技术。  
每一代，都会解决新的问题。

然而，在修改任何 **架构**、**工作流**、**功能**、**人工智能** 之前，  
都应首先回答一个问题：

> **「这是否让 ClearStrata 更接近自己的使命？」**

如果答案是否定的，那么，这个方案就 **值得重新思考**。

因为，真正的进步，并不是增加了多少功能，  
也不是采用了多先进的技术，  
更不是人工智能变得多么聪明。

真正的进步，在于平台是否更加忠于 **它最初存在的意义**。

**技术服务使命。**  
**使命服务社区。**  
**社区塑造文明。**

---

Every generation has the right to innovate.  
No generation has the right to forget why we began.

每一代人都有 **创新** 的权利。  
但没有任何一代人，有权 **忘记我们为何出发**。
