## 1. Purpose

Review the **implementation readiness** of **IU-2.2 — Resolution Snapshot Materialization** against the approved implementation design ([`E-02-IU-2.2-Implementation.md`](E-02-IU-2.2-Implementation.md)) and Design Review ([`E-02-IU-2.2-Design-Review.md`](E-02-IU-2.2-Design-Review.md)).
