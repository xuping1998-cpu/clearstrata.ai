## 22. Operational authority paths (locked)

### Lost ack

RU-1.2 txn commits → response lost → later evaluator fresh-reads DB → C1–C8 visible → C9 true → **COMMITTED**

### Normal success

Even after `ATOMIC_ENVELOPE_COMPLETE`, formal COMMITTED **only** through same evaluator fresh-read path.

### IDEMPOTENT_RETURN

May indicate durable envelope exists — evaluator **still independently re-runs C1–C9**. Term itself ≠ COMMITTED evidence.

### Rollback

No complete durable C1–C8 → **NO_FREEZE** (no footprint) or **FAIL_CLOSED** (partial unexpected footprint) — never COMMITTED.
