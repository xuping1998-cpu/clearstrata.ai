# Founding Documents Registry

## 创始文献登记册

The Founding Documents Registry is the **official record** of all constitutional-level documents within the ClearStrata repository.

It preserves the **identity**, **history**, **constitutional authority**, and **institutional memory** of the platform.

**The Registry itself is a permanent constitutional record.**

- Entries may be **added**.
- Existing entries shall **never be renumbered**.
- **Document numbers are permanent.**

---

《创始文献登记册》用于登记 ClearStrata 所有具有宪章级别的正式文献。

登记册记录平台的：**身份、历史、宪章权威、制度记忆**。

登记册本身属于 **平台永久治理记录**。

- 未来可以 **增加** 新的创始文献。
- 已经分配的编号 **永不修改**。
- 编号具有 **永久性**。

## Registry Principles

- **Document numbers are permanent.**
- **Documents may evolve.**
- **History shall never be erased.**
- Every amendment shall preserve the **historical integrity** of earlier documents.
- **The Registry itself** is part of the institutional memory of ClearStrata.

---

## Founding Principle

Every generation **may add** new documents.  
No generation **may rewrite** the historical foundation upon which they were built.

---

**创始原则**

每一代人 **都可以留下** 新的文献。  
但没有任何一代人 **可以重写** 自己所继承的历史基石。
