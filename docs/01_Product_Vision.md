## What ClearStrata Is

ClearStrata is a **community governance platform** for strata corporations, HOAs, and similar property communities.

It is **not** merely a building-management tool.  
It exists to help neighbors **discuss**, **decide**, **vote**, **execute**, **audit**, and **remember** — together.

---

## Mission

Help every community govern itself with **transparency**, **accountability**, **participation**, and **trust**.

帮助每一个社区，实现公开透明、责任明确、共同参与、彼此信任的共同治理。

**Meta Rule II — Never Lose the Why:** Before any architecture, workflow, feature, or AI change, ask — *Does this bring ClearStrata closer to its Mission?* See [`00_ClearStrata_Constitution.md`](00_ClearStrata_Constitution.md) — Meta Rules.

---

## Vision

Preserve the **governance memory** of every community, so future generations can build on the wisdom of those before them.

保存每一个社区的治理记忆，帮助未来每一代人，站在前人的智慧之上。

## Who We Serve

| Stakeholder | Need |
|-------------|------|
| **Owners** | Understand decisions, participate in votes, access records |
| **Council / strata board** | Run meetings, resolutions, notices, audits |
| **Property managers** | Operate workflows without breaking transparency |
| **Future owners** | Inherit complete, searchable governance history |

---

## Product Scope (High Level)

Capabilities must map to the **Governance Lifecycle** (Article VI):

| Phase | Product examples |
|-------|------------------|
| Discover | Alerts, dashboards, compliance signals |
| Discuss | Agendas, owner discussion, notices |
| Summarize | AI-assisted meeting/minutes summaries |
| Resolve | Resolutions, council actions |
| Meet | AGM, SGM, council meetings |
| Vote | Owner voting, elections, written votes |
| Execute | Tasks, procurement, finance follow-through |
| Audit | Logs, reports, invoice audit trails |
| Archive | Frozen snapshots, archived meetings, permanent records |

Features outside this lifecycle require explicit Constitution Review justification.

---

## What We Do Not Optimize For

Per **Rule 5 (No Constitutional Regression)**, we do not sacrifice:

- Transparency for speed
- Participation for convenience
- Accountability for automation
- Community memory for storage cost

---

## Success Measures

A feature succeeds when it:

1. Makes an important decision **more transparent** (Article I)
2. Preserves **community memory** (Article VII)
3. Strengthens **trust** through traceability
4. Leaves the community **better governed** than before (Article VIII)
