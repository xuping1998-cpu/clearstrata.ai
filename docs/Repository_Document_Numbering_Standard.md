## Purpose

This standard establishes the **permanent document numbering system** for the ClearStrata repository.

Its purpose is to preserve:

- **Constitutional authority**
- **Institutional memory**
- **Historical continuity**
- **Governance transparency**
- **Long-term maintainability**

Every constitutional-level document shall receive a **permanent document number**.

- Document numbers are **unique**.
- Document numbers are **never reused**.
- Document numbers are **never renumbered**.
- **Historical references must remain valid forever.**

---

## Repository Resolution

This document is hereby **adopted** as the official document numbering standard of the ClearStrata Repository.

Beginning with **Constitution Version 1.0**, all future:

- Constitutional documents
- Governance documents
- Standards
- Project records
- Architecture records
- Permanent repository documents

shall follow this numbering system.

**CS-001 becomes the governing standard for repository documentation.**

---

### 正式决议

自《平台宪章》Version 1.0 起，**CS-001 正式成为** ClearStrata Repository **唯一正式文档编号规范**。

未来所有正式治理文档、宪章文档、项目文档、架构文档、标准文档，**均应遵循 CS-001**。

---

## Scope

CS-001 applies to every **official document** maintained within the ClearStrata repository, including but not limited to:

- Founding Documents
- Constitutional Amendments
- Governance Principles
- Constitutional Standards
- AI Standards
- Governance Casebooks
- Constitution Reviews
- Architecture Records
- Project Records
- Repository Management Documents
- Official Templates

Documents **outside this scope** (e.g. temporary notes, drafts, meeting notes, experiments) are **not required** to use permanent numbering.

---

### 实施范围

CS-001 适用于：**FD、CA、GP、CS、AI、GC、CR、AR、PR、RM、TM** 类正式文档。

临时草稿、会议记录、实验文档、个人笔记 **无需永久编号**。

## Numbering Rules

1. Every document receives **only one** permanent identifier.
2. Numbers are **never reused**.
3. Numbers are **never renumbered**.
4. **Deleted** documents remain **reserved**.
5. **Historical citations** remain valid **forever**.
6. Projects may finish. **Document numbers never retire.**
7. The **Registry** records history. It **never rewrites** history.

---

## Implementation Rule

**Effective immediately** (2026-06-29):

1. Every newly created **official document** shall receive a permanent identifier.
2. Every permanent identifier shall **conform to CS-001**.
3. Every official document header shall **conform to [CS-002](Document_Identity_Block_Standard.md)**.
4. Every official document structure shall **conform to [CS-003](Repository_Document_Layout_Standard.md)**.
5. Existing permanent document identifiers shall **never be renumbered**.
6. **Retired** document numbers remain **reserved**.
7. Historical references must remain valid **indefinitely**.
