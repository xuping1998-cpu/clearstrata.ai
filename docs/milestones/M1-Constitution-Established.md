## What M1 establishes

1. **RC000** is the supreme design principle for all future RC work.
2. **CDGL** (Issue → Governance → Meeting → Voting → Execution → Accountability → Continuous Improvement) is the mandatory product loop.
3. **CGE** is the official engineering methodology: Constitution → Architecture → Requirements → Implementation.
4. **RC010** is aligned to the Meeting layer — *Meeting Resolution Authoring Before Snapshot* — with Governance resolution authoring retired.
5. **MGS (Appendix A)** governs how all milestones are recorded permanently.
