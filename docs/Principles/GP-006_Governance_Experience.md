## Purpose

Community governance should **not** require training.

The **Governance Hub** exists to help every property member understand the current state of community governance **naturally**.

Good governance should be **visible**.

- Not hidden inside menus.
- Not hidden inside documents.
- Not hidden inside procedures.

A first-time user should understand the governance process **within five seconds**.

---

## Constitutional Principle

Governance is not merely a sequence of actions.

Governance is a **shared public process**.

The interface shall present governance as a **living process** rather than a collection of pages.

Users should always understand:

| Question | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **What** is happening? | Current state of community governance |
| **Why** is it happening? | Constitutional and community context |
| **What** comes next? | Lifecycle and next constitutional step |
| **What** should I do? | Role-appropriate participation |

— **without reading documentation.**

---

## Four Constitutional Questions

Every Governance Hub page shall answer four questions.

### 1. What is happening?

- Current discussions
- Current consultations
- Current resolutions
- Current meetings
- Current voting

### 2. Why is it happening?

- Constitutional basis
- Applicable bylaws
- Relevant community context
- AI constitutional explanation (advisory only)

### 3. What comes next?

Lifecycle visualization:

```
Discussion
    ↓
Consultation
    ↓
Resolution
    ↓
Meeting
    ↓
Voting
    ↓
Archive
```

### 4. What should I do?

| Role | Actions |
|------|---------|
| **Owner** | Read · Comment · Vote · Follow |
| **Council** | Review · Generate CDA · Prepare Resolution · Schedule Meeting |
| **Platform** | Observe · Audit · Preserve transparency |

## Governance Experience Principles

### Principle 1 — Show Process

Never show isolated pages.

Always show where the current matter is inside the **governance lifecycle**.

### Principle 2 — Show Progress

Every governance matter must display:

- Current stage
- Remaining actions
- Next constitutional step

### Principle 3 — Show Purpose

Every action should explain **why** it exists.

Buttons without constitutional purpose should not exist.

### Principle 4 — Different Roles

One Governance Hub. Different responsibilities.

Owners and Council members share the **same governance space**.

Only available actions differ.

*(See [GP-005 — Shared Governance Space](GP-005_Shared_Governance_Space.md).)*

### Principle 5 — Transparency First

- Discussion is public.
- Revisions are traceable.
- AI is advisory.
- Decisions belong to people.

---

## Governance Golden Rule

Users should **never** ask:

> *"Where do I click next?"*

The interface should naturally answer:

- **This is where your community is.**
- **This is what is happening.**
- **This is what comes next.**
- **This is how you participate.**

---

## Design Goal

The Governance Hub should explain the current state of community governance **without requiring the user to read documentation**.

A first-time user should understand **within five seconds**:

1. What is happening?
2. Why is it happening?
3. What comes next?
4. What should I do?

If those four questions are answered, the Governance Hub has fulfilled its **constitutional purpose**.

## 中文版

### 治理体验

社区治理不应需要培训。

治理中心（Governance Hub）应让每一位物业成员**自然理解**当前社区治理状态。

良好的治理应当**可见**——不藏在菜单里、文件里或流程里。

首次访问者应在**五秒内**理解治理过程。

### 四个宪章问题

每个治理中心页面必须回答：

1. **正在发生什么？**
2. **为什么发生？**
3. **下一步是什么？**
4. **我应当做什么？**

### 治理黄金法则

用户不应问：「我下一步点哪里？」

界面应自然回答：社区在哪里、正在发生什么、下一步是什么、如何参与。
