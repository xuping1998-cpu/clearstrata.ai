## Purpose

**Community Resolution** is the constitutional bridge between public deliberation and formal decision-making.

It transforms community discussion into an organized, reviewable, and transparent proposal.

---

## Fundamental Principle

A **Resolution** represents the community's best current understanding.

It does **NOT** represent the community's final decision.

Only a legitimate meeting and a legitimate vote can produce a binding decision.

## Purpose of a Resolution

A Resolution exists to organize, clarify, and prepare community decisions.

| It exists to… | It does not… |
|---------------|--------------|
| Reduce misunderstanding | Replace debate |
| Improve decisions | Replace legitimate decision-making |

Every Community Resolution shall include:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Resolution Title | Official title of the proposal |
| Executive Summary | Plain-language summary |
| Constitutional Basis | Applicable constitutional principles |
| Supporting Governance Matter(s) | Linked matter record(s) |
| Supporting Discussion(s) | Deliberation reference |
| Supporting Documents | Evidence and attachments |
| CDA Summary Reference | Optional CDA report link |
| Council Review Status | Review stage and outcome |
| Meeting Reference | Optional linked meeting |
| Voting Method | Optional proposed method |
| Revision History | Append-only change log |

---

## Council Responsibilities

**Council may:**

- Review, clarify, revise, and prepare Community Resolutions

**Council may NOT:**

- Remove documented community opinions

**Council must:**

- Preserve constitutional transparency

---

## Role of AI

The **Constitutional Deliberation Assistant** may:

- Suggest, summarize, compare, identify risks, prepare draft resolutions

**AI shall NEVER:**

- Approve, adopt, or publish Community Resolutions

---

## Transparency

Every Resolution shall identify its origin.

**Example:**

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Origin Governance Matter | Matter #102 |
| Origin Discussion | 128 comments |
| CDA Report | Analysis #4 |
| Council Review | Revision #2 |

---

## Revision Principle

Community Resolutions are living documents.

Every revision must remain visible. Nothing shall be overwritten. **Revision History is permanent.**

## Repository Principle

A Resolution does not end discussion.

It concludes one stage of governance, and prepares the next.

**Downstream:** Meeting and voting principles — [PR-005 Meeting & Voting Integration](../projects/PR-005_Meeting_Voting_Integration_Phase_5.md) (Voting Golden Rule, Meeting Constitutional Rule).

## Closing Statement

A good Resolution does not tell the community what to think.

It helps the community understand **what it is deciding**.

## 中文版

### 社区决议

Community Resolution **不是最终决定**。

它是社区公开讨论之后、依法进入正式会议之前，形成的一份 **公开、透明、可审查、可修订、可追溯** 的正式治理文件。

它代表的是社区当前形成的最佳共识，而不是最终法律决定。

真正具有法律效力的决定，只能由 **合法会议** 和 **合法投票** 产生。

## Golden Principle

**A Resolution clarifies what the community is preparing to decide.**  
**It never decides on behalf of the community.**

**Community Resolution 帮助社区更清楚地知道自己即将决定什么。**  
**它永远不会代替社区作出决定。**

---

## Resolution Golden Rule

### 决议黄金法则

A Resolution earns legitimacy through **transparency**, not authority.

It is trusted because its reasoning, its evidence, its revisions, and its origins remain visible to the community.

**Authority may prepare a Resolution.**  
**Only transparency can earn the community's trust.**

---

Community Resolution 的合法性，来自公开透明，而不是来自权力本身。

社区之所以信任一份决议，不是因为它是谁写的，也不是因为它是谁批准的，而是因为：

- 它的形成过程始终公开
- 它的依据始终可查
- 它的修订历史始终保留
- 它的来源始终可追溯

权力可以准备一份决议。**只有透明，才能赢得社区的信任。**

## Resolution Inscription

### 决议铭文

A Resolution does not become legitimate when it is written.

It becomes legitimate when every member can understand **how it came to exist**.

---

一份决议，并不会因为已经写出来就拥有合法性。

真正赋予它合法性的，是社区中的每一个人，都能够理解：它为什么形成，如何形成，又依据什么形成。
