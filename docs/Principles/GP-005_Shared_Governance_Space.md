## Purpose

Community governance should remain **visible**, **continuous**, and **shared**.

Governance shall **not** be separated into different worlds for different roles.

Instead, everyone participates in the **same governance space**, with **different responsibilities**.

---

## Core Principle

**One Community. One Governance Space. Different Responsibilities. Not Different Worlds.**

---

## Design Principle

**Community Deliberation** is the **Governance Hub**.

| Role | Participation |
|------|----------------|
| **Owners** | Discussion, consultation, feedback, and voting |
| **Council** | Governance, revision, resolution, meeting preparation, and constitutional workflow |

Everyone remains inside the **same public governance space**.

---

## Council Workspace

**Council Workspace** is **NOT** an independent module.

It is the **governance panel** of Community Deliberation.

Its purpose is to **advance** community governance — not to **isolate** governance.

| Layer | Role |
|-------|------|
| **Governance Hub** (`/community-deliberation`) | Constitutional home of governance — shared by all roles |
| **Governance Panel** (right side, council roles) | Entrance — summary, actions, publish, open workspace |
| **Council Workspace** (`/council/workspace`) | Detailed operating page — opened from the Governance Panel |

---

## Owner Experience

Owners shall see their own **Participation Panel**.

Examples:

- My Comments
- My Votes
- Followed Matters
- Subscribed Discussions

Owners should always feel they are participating in the **same governance process**.

---

## Council Experience

Council shall see **Governance Panel**.

Examples:

- Today's Actions
- Active Matters
- Generate CDA
- Create Resolution
- Schedule Meeting
- Open Voting
- Archive Matter

---

## Constitutional Interpretation

Transparency does **not** require everyone to perform the same work.

Transparency **requires** everyone to see the **same governance process**.

---

## UI Principle

Different roles may have **different tools**.

They shall **not** have **different realities**.

---

## Repository Principle

**Community Deliberation** is the constitutional home of governance.

**Council Workspace** is one role's working area **inside** that home.

---

## Permanent Inscription

A community should never wonder **where governance happens**.

Governance should happen **where the community already is**.

---

## 中文版

### 共享治理空间

治理应当发生在社区共同可见的空间。  
而不是发生在只有管理者才能进入的后台。

社区成员与业委会共同处于**同一个治理空间**。  
不同角色，承担不同职责；  
而不是进入不同世界。

---

### 设计原则

《社区议事厅》正式成为 **Governance Hub（治理中心）**。

业委会工作台成为社区议事厅内部的 **Governance Panel（治理面板）**。

它不是独立模块。它属于社区议事厅。

---

### 永久设计原则

一个社区。  
一个治理空间。  
不同职责。  
不是不同世界。

---

### 宪章解释

真正的公开治理，并不是所有人做同样的事情。

而是所有人共同看到**同一个治理过程**。
